Grade & Specifications of Stainless Steel Pipes

Every project asks for a specific material and a specific standard. We offer stainless steel pipes across a wide range of grades, so the pipe you order suits the job, whether that is a hot process line or a corrosive coastal site.

We make welded pipes, seamless pipes and custom-shaped tubes in austenitic stainless steel grades, with other grades on request, and every pipe is held to the dimensional tolerances its specification sets.

Packing Excellence

A pipe is only as good as the condition it arrives in. We protect quality from the day a pipe leaves our plant until it reaches you, with extra care for long, thin-walled tubes and shapes such as U-bends that can deform in transit.

Our packing process includes:

  • Customised bundling for stable, easy handling.
  • Protective end caps against knocks to the pipe ends.
  • Corrosion-prevention measures for long journeys.
  • Secure fastening so bundles do not shift or take impact.

Packing follows the shipping requirements of the destination, so the order arrives undamaged.

Stainless Steel Pipes FAQs

What types of stainless steel pipes do you manufacture?

We manufacture welded pipes, seamless pipes, decorative tubes and custom-shaped stainless steel pipes, in a range of grades and finishes.

How do seamless pipes differ from welded pipes?

Seamless pipe has no weld seam, which makes it the usual choice for high-pressure, high-temperature and corrosive service. Welded pipe is formed from strip and welded along its length. It suits low- to medium-pressure lines, construction and ornamental work, and it is usually the more economical option.

Which grades do you offer?

Austenitic stainless steel grades such as 202, 304, 304L, 316 and 316L. The full list is in the grades table above. Other grades can be made to your requirement.

Can I order custom sizes, lengths and shapes?

Yes. We make pipes to each client's own requirement, in custom sizes and shapes. Send your drawing or specification with the quantity and we will confirm what we can make.

Which industries use your pipes?

Oil and gas, mining, water and waste management, marine engineering, chemical and petrochemical plants, power generation, pulp and paper, process piping and pharmaceutical facilities.

Do you provide third-party testing?

Yes. We provide third-party testing. Let us know the inspection agency and the tests you need when you place the enquiry.

Are you ISO certified?

Yes. SD Prime Steels LLP is certified to ISO 9001:2015.

What should I send to get a quotation?

The grade, standard, outside diameter, wall thickness or schedule, length, quantity and any test or certificate requirements. You can use the enquiry form, call us, or send the details by email.
